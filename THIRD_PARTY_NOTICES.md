## Audio

- **Car Engine Loop 96kHz, 4s**, by **qubodup (Ivan Gabovitch)**. Used under **CC BY 3.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-loop-96khz-4s. Normalized recording selected from source archive; looped with variable playback pitch in game. This is the attribution required by that license.
- **Midnight Drive**, by **congusbongus**, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/midnight-drive
- **chiptune police loop**, by **obscure music**, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/chiptune-police-loop
- **Free Music Pack**, by **Alexander Ehlers (tricksntraps)**, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/free-music-pack. Seven unmodified MP3 tracks: Doomed, Flags, Great mission, Spacetime, Twists, Waking the devil, Warped. Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Alexander%20Ehlers%20-%20Free%20Music%20Pack.zip. License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
- **Sirens and Alarm Noise**, by **aquinn**, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/sirens-and-alarm-noise. Eight-second excerpt converted to WAV.
- **Helicopter Sounds**, by **aquinn**, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/helicopter-sounds. Eight-second excerpt converted to WAV.
- **The Free Firearm Sound Library**, by **Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney**, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library. Selected 1911, 1917, AK-47, AR-15, Carl Gustav M45 and Mossberg recordings trimmed and converted to WAV. Weapon sound assignments are approximations: M45 supplies both Uzi and MP5 sounds, and 1917 supplies the heavy-pistol sound.

Horn, tire-friction effects and the electric drivetrain hum are synthesized in game. Explosion playback currently reuses an existing impact/gunshot recording as a placeholder.

Modern branded names in the garage identify original stylistic variations of the licensed unbranded sedan and SUV. These are not exact manufacturer models, scans or official licensed vehicle designs. No manufacturer badges or logos are imported.
